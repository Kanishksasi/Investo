Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

We do not sell your personal data. We never have, and we never will.

This Privacy Policy explains how Bruxel Academy LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information from users of the Investo. mobile application (“App”). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Investo, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication)
  • Profile data: Username, XP points, level, completed lessons, and learning progress
  • Usage data: Lessons viewed, quiz results, flashcard sessions, streak data, and in-app activity
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, and app version for diagnostics
  • Parental consent records (accounts under 13 only): a parent or guardian's email address, the timestamp they approved the account, and the consent request itself — kept only as long as needed to document that approval

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and personalize your learning experience in the App
  • Track your progress, XP, levels, and lesson completion
  • Maintain your account and restore data across devices
  • Improve the App through aggregated, anonymized analytics
  • Send important account-related communications (not marketing spam)
  • Provide customer support when you contact us

Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services to operate the App:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication, cloud database (Firestore), photo storage (Cloud Storage), and analytics. Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google’s data processing terms. Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Gemini API: The AI chat feature (“Mr. Investo”) sends your questions to Google’s Gemini API to generate a response. We do not send your name or other identifying details with these requests.
  • Groq: Used as an automatic backup for the AI chat feature if Gemini is temporarily unavailable, under the same data-handling practice as above.
  • Resend: Used only to deliver the parental-consent email for accounts under 13 — it never sees the child’s learning data, only the parent’s email address for that one message.

Children’s Privacy

Investo is designed for users ages 8 and older, and we comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) for users under 13.

Before we ever collect an under-13 user’s email or any other personal information, our app asks for age first. If someone identifies as under 13, we do not create an account or collect anything about them until a parent or guardian has verifiably consented: we email the parent a description of exactly what will be collected (name, age, learning activity, and that AI-tutor chat messages are sent to Google’s Gemini API) and a link they must actively click to approve — not just receive. Only after that click does account creation proceed.

A parent or guardian can, at any time, ask us to show them what we’ve collected about their child, have it deleted, or revoke consent entirely (which deletes the account) — contact sasikumark@bentonvillek12.org. If a consent request isn’t approved within 7 days, the request and any information collected for it are automatically deleted and no account is created.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13 without consent, contact us immediately at the address above and we will delete it promptly.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sasikumark@bentonvillek12.org.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), Firebase’s built-in security rules, and secure authentication. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us
