Common questions, answered plainly.

Still stuck? The Help Center has more, or email investo.team@bruxelacademy.com.

Is investo. really free?

Yes. Every lesson, the AI tutor, and the practice investing portfolio are free. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

Is the investing real?

No. The portfolio uses $10,000 of fictional practice money with real market prices. No real money is ever involved, and there’s nothing to deposit or withdraw.

Is my child’s data safe?

We collect the minimum data needed to run the app and never sell personal information to third parties. If your child is under 13, no account is created until you approve it by email. See our Privacy Policy for the full details, or email investo.team@bruxelacademy.com anytime to review or delete their data.

What ages is investo. built for?

investo. is built for ages 8-18, with content that scales from simple saving and spending basics for younger kids up through budgeting, credit, and investing fundamentals for teens. It’s useful at any age.

Is investo. available on Android?

Yes. investo. is on Google Play for Android 8.0 or later, and on the App Store for iOS 18 or later. A few features (flashcard decks, Daily Challenges, and the Course Map) are iOS only for now.
